About the clinic

A calmer, clearer way to receive medical care

Medora Clinic was founded by a group of physicians who believed patients deserve more than a ten-minute appointment. Today, our multidisciplinary team brings family medicine and eight specialties together so your care is coordinated, not fragmented.

Our mission

To deliver accurate, compassionate and accessible care that treats the whole person, not just the symptom.

Our vision

To be the most trusted neighborhood clinic, where every family feels informed, respected and safe.

  • Integrity in every diagnosis
  • Respect for your time and privacy
  • Evidence-based treatment
  • Compassion at every visit
